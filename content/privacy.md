# Privacy

## The website

The site sets no cookies and loads no analytics, advertising, font or script from another domain. The instruction file generator runs in your browser. What you type stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

The company that hosts the site may log requests, including IP addresses, as any web host does. The host will be named here before the site goes public.

## The newsletter

The signup form is switched off until a newsletter provider is configured. When it is on, the only data we ask for is the email address you type, and it is sent to the provider only when you submit the form.

Signup will use double opt-in: you receive an email and must confirm before you get anything. Every email will carry an unsubscribe link. The provider and the person responsible for the list will be named here before the form is enabled.

## Contact

A contact address for privacy requests will be listed here before the site goes public.
