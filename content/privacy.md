The site sets no cookies and loads no analytics, advertising, font or script from another domain. The instruction file generator runs in your browser. What you type stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

## The newsletter

The signup form is switched off until the newsletter opens. This section describes what will apply once it is on.

- What we collect. The email address you type. Our provider also records the IP address and referrer-level details of your signup.
- Why. To send you the weekly digest of changes in AI coding tools. We do not use the address for anything else, we do not sell it and we do not share it, other than with the provider below.
- Legal basis. Your consent (GDPR, article 6(1)(a)). You give it by submitting the form and confirming through the link in the first email, which is a double opt-in. You get nothing before you confirm.
- Withdrawing. Every email carries an unsubscribe link. You can also write to {{privacyEmail}}. Withdrawing does not affect what was done before.
- Provider. Buttondown, LLC, in the United States, sends the emails and stores the list on our behalf. Its [data processing agreement](https://buttondown.com/legal/data-processing-agreement) uses the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses for transfers from the European Economic Area, and its [sub-processors](https://buttondown.com/legal/subprocessors) are listed on its site. The signup and confirmation pages are hosted by Buttondown, and its own policies apply to them.
- Opens and clicks. We do not track them. Our emails carry no tracking pixel, and the links in them are not rewritten.
- How long. We keep your address for as long as you stay subscribed. When you unsubscribe, or earlier if you ask us, we delete your data within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep it. Copies in the provider's backups expire on its own schedule, which its [documentation](https://docs.buttondown.com/deleting-and-recovering-data) gives as up to two days.

## Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, for its correction or erasure, for a limit on its use, for a copy you can take elsewhere, and you can object to its use. You can withdraw your consent at any time. Write to [{{privacyEmail}}](mailto:{{privacyEmail}}). We answer within one month.

If you think your rights are not respected, you can complain to the CNIL, the French data protection authority ([complaint form](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/plaintes)), or to the authority of your own country in the European Union.
