# Privacy

## Who is responsible

For the newsletter, the person responsible for personal data (responsable de traitement, in the sense of the GDPR) and its publisher is Thomas Rambaut. The same person is responsible for the messages sent to the contact addresses and for the request logs described in the next section. No postal address is published. The only contact is [{{privacyEmail}}](mailto:{{privacyEmail}}). For any question or request about your data, write to that address.

## The website

The site sets no cookies and loads no advertising or font from another domain. The only script from another domain is Cloudflare Web Analytics, described below. The instruction file generator runs in your browser. What you type stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

The company that hosts the site may log requests, including IP addresses, as any web host does. The host is {{hostName}}, {{hostAddress}}. Its [data processing addendum](https://www.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-customer-dpa/) covers transfers from the European Economic Area to the United States with the Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, and its [privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) applies to those logs. Apart from the visit counts below, this site adds no tracking on top of them.

To count visits, Cloudflare adds its Web Analytics script, loaded from static.cloudflareinsights.com, to each page as it is served. The script measures how long the page took to load and sends that, with the page address and the address of the page you came from, to this site's /cdn-cgi/rum address, which Cloudflare runs. According to Cloudflare, Web Analytics leaves out query strings, uses no cookies or local storage, does not fingerprint visitors by IP address or browser, does not track individual users across sites, and does not collect or use visitors' personal data ([product page](https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/), [documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/about/), [FAQ](https://developers.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/faq/)). We see totals only: page views, visits, load times, and counts by country, page, referring site, browser, operating system and device type. We use them to know how many people read the site and which pages they read, and to give sponsors audience figures. Our dashboard shows the last six months.

## The newsletter

- What we collect. The email address you type. {{nlRecords}}

Its [data processing agreement](https://buttondown.com/legal/data-processing-agreement) uses the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses for transfers from the European Economic Area, and its [sub-processors](https://buttondown.com/legal/subprocessors) are listed on its site. {{nlHostedPages}}
- Opens and clicks. We do not track them. {{nlNoTracking}}

If you sign up and never confirm, we delete the address within 40 days. Deleting means removing the record from the provider's database, not only hiding it. Copies in the provider's backups expire on its own schedule, which its [documentation](https://docs.buttondown.com/deleting-and-recovering-data) gives as up to two days.

## Messages you send us

If you write to {{contactEmail}}, {{privacyEmail}} or {{sponsorContact}}, your message reaches Thomas Rambaut through Cloudflare Email Routing and a private mailbox. We use it only to answer you, on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering (GDPR, article 6(1)(f)), and we keep it for 12 months after the exchange ends, then we delete it.

## Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, for its correction or erasure, for a limit on its use, for a copy you can take elsewhere, and you can object to its use. You can withdraw your consent at any time. Write to [{{privacyEmail}}](mailto:{{privacyEmail}}). We answer within one month.

If you think your rights are not respected, you can complain to the CNIL, the French data protection authority ([complaint form](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/plaintes)), or to the authority of your own country in the European Union.
